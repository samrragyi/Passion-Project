Important Information & Privacy

  • Educational Symptom Tracker Only: This tool is an educational symptom tracking aid created to help you prepare for a consultation with your doctor. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.
  • Clinical Guidance: Questioning is framed around the validated Greene Climacteric Scale (GCS) and aligns with National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE NG23) guidelines on perimenopause and menopause assessment.
  • 100% Data Privacy: This tool does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable data or health records. All inputs remain strictly on your device during this session and are deleted as soon as you close your browser.
  • Medical Emergencies: If you are experiencing severe pain, unusual bleeding, severe sudden chest pain, or mental health crisis, please contact your local doctor or emergency services straight away (for example, NHS 111 or 999 in the UK).
